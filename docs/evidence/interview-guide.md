# Interview guide (semi-structured, 30-45 min)

**Before:** explain the project, that participation is voluntary, notes are anonymised, no player names are recorded. Ask consent.

## Current practice
1. How do you currently plan and record training sessions? What tools (paper, Excel, apps)?
2. Which player indicators do you track (tests, attendance, load, wellness, match stats)? How often?
3. How do you decide what an individual player should work on next?
4. How do you account for age differences and growth / maturity within one age group?

## Pain points
5. What is hardest or most time-consuming in monitoring players today?
6. Have you used any app or wearable? What did you like / dislike? Cost?

## The proposed system
7. Walk through prototype screens (`prototype/wireframes.html`). What is unclear or missing?
8. What recommendations would be useful? What would you not trust?
9. What explanation do you need to accept a recommendation?
10. What is the maximum time you would spend on data entry per session?

## Constraints
11. Data protection: parental consent, who may see player data?
12. Language (RU / KZ / EN), devices (phone vs laptop), internet connectivity at the field?

## Close
13. Would you be willing to take part in a pilot / testing (anonymised data)? Whom else should we talk to?

**After:** write notes within 24 h in `interviews/I<n>.md` using the template: context, key statements (quotes), requirements derived, assumptions confirmed/refuted.
